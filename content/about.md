I am an economist and Data Scientist with experience in credit risk, portfolio analysis, and financial analytics in the financial sector (fintech and banking).

I combine **statistical modeling with business judgment**: I translate models into decisions about portfolio risk, lending, and profitability.

## How I work

I manage the full life cycle of credit models: data preparation, training, validation, production, monitoring of risk indicators, and recalibration. I apply it to origination, renewals, credit behavior, early warnings, and fraud, together with financial forecasting, scenario evaluation, and the automation of analytical processes.
