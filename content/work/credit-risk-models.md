## The problem

A fintech lender needs to decide who receives credit, and on what terms, at every stage of the customer relationship: the first application, the first loans, and each renewal. Each stage has different information and different risk.

## My role

I develop, implement, and monitor the credit risk models for origination, first loans, and renewals at Solventa.

## Technical approach

- Models trained with XGBoost, Random Forest, and Neural Networks.
- Feature development and behavioral analysis to segment risk profiles and identify warning signals and atypical patterns, with early work on early warning and fraud detection models.
- Variable definition and validation together with Data Engineering, ensuring consistency between training and production.

## Monitoring

Models in production are monitored with discrimination and stability indicators (**AUC, KS, PSI**), with periodic backtesting and recalibration.
