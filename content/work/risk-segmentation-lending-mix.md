## The problem

A single approval policy treats very different customers the same way. The portfolio needed to grow where risk was low and stay protected where delinquency was high.

## My role

I manage the credit portfolio in Peru, with about **US$5 million** in capital, achieving positive profitability.

## Approach

- Risk segmentation analysis to reconfigure the disbursement mix.
- Higher approval in the lowest-delinquency segment and tighter restrictions in the highest-delinquency segment.
- Differentiated models by segment.
- Portfolio, risk, placement, and profitability analysis, with forecasting and scenario evaluation to support credit strategy decisions.
